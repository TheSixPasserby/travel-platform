## GitHub及搜索失败

本地起点和远程main均为e1ef92c7c7548db6417336aad0ed2299713cc574。核查时最新运行43371817022的后端277项中两项失败，后续步骤跳过。失败方法为M16SearchApiTest的anonymousSearchCombinesFivePublicKindsAndInternalLinksWithSafeFields和literalWildcardUnicodeEmptyAndInvalidInputsAreHandled。[原运行](https://github.com/TheSixPasserby/travel-platform/actions/runs/43371817022)
